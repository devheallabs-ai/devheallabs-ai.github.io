Our rule on numbers

Measured, or absent

We publish a figure only when we measured it ourselves under a stated method, and we say what that method was. Where a number has not been measured that way, the page says so instead of estimating.

What this rules out. No ROI figures. No productivity multipliers. No cost-saving percentages. No throughput comparisons against products we have not tested. If you see a number on this site, we produced it and can tell you how.
