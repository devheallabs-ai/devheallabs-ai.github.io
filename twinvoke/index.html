Assurance workflow

Mirror. Simulate. Verify. Invoke.

The intended workflow keeps enterprise reality in view from proposed action through accountable execution.

01 / MIRROR

Build the twin

Synchronise topology, signals, dependencies, identities, and authority boundaries.

02 / SIMULATE

Branch the future

Model proposed actions and failure scenarios without changing the live environment.

03 / VERIFY

Check the contract

Evaluate invariants, policy rules, risk, and predicted blast radius.

04 / INVOKE

Act with control

Allow, require approval, or block execution while recording the decision evidence.
