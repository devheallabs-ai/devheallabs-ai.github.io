Design Partner Programme

You would be among the first

We are not going to pretend otherwise. We have technology, a clear problem thesis and no reference customers. A design partnership is how those become evidence — the engagement produces the proof rather than requiring it up front.

Start the conversation See where the evidence stands
Why this instead of a sales process

A normal enterprise sale needs proof we do not have

Procurement asks who else uses this. We cannot answer that yet. Rather than dress up a demo as a deployment, we would rather invert it: pick one real, bounded problem, agree what success looks like before starting, and find out honestly whether the technology helps.

If it does not, that is a useful answer and we would tell you.

How it works

Six steps, no surprises

1 · Pick one problem

One real, bounded problem you already have. Not a survey of everything we could theoretically do.

2 · Define success first

In your metric, written down before we start, so it cannot be moved afterwards to make the result look better.

3 · Constrained pilot

The smallest deployment that can produce a real signal. Where actions are involved, read-only or shadow mode first.

4 · Measure

Against step two. Including when the answer is that it did not help.

5 · Decide

Continue, stop or adjust. No obligation to continue, and no pressure to.

6 · Publish — only if you agree

Written consent, and only figures you confirm. You can decline entirely and still keep the work.

Fit

Who this suits — and who it does not

Good fit

Teams who can decide quickly

Technical founders, platform leads, SRE teams with a specific recurring cost. Organisations comfortable evaluating something early on its technical merits.

Not yet

Procurement-led evaluations

If your process requires customer references, a support SLA and a completed security questionnaire, we cannot meet that today. Worth revisiting later.
