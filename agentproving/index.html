Common questions

Bring an agent. Leave with evidence.

How is this different from a prompt benchmark?

D-AgentProving evaluates multi-step decisions, tool calls, state mutations, failures, recovery, and cost across a complete run.

Does the demo require production credentials?

The proving workflow uses controlled digital-twin services, allowing the agent to act without touching live customer systems or production data.

What should we bring to a demo?

Bring one agent, its intended tools, and the workflow or failure mode you need to prove. We will map it to a focused simulation and review the resulting evidence.
