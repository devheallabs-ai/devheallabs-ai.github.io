Engagements

Scoped work, not open-ended consulting

Each engagement states what you get and what it produces. We have deliberately not attached fixed durations or prices, because we would be inventing them — scope is agreed per engagement.

Available now

Built on technology you can already verify

Available

AI Opportunity Assessment

For: engineering leaders deciding where AI is worth applying.
You get: a written assessment of candidate problems, ranked by feasibility against effort, with the ones we think are a bad idea named explicitly.
Produces: a decision, not a proposal.

Available

Private AI Evaluation

For: teams blocked by data residency or contractual restriction.
You get: local inference running in your environment on a representative task, with an honest read on whether output quality is sufficient for it.
Produces: a go / no-go backed by your own data.

Available

Agent Architecture Review

For: teams deploying agents with access to systems that matter.
You get: a review of your agent design against the influence-governance questions in our research — who acts, what they may reach, and how much one input can shape a privileged action.
Produces: a risk picture. Vendor-neutral; the fixes are usually in your architecture, not our products.

Available

Custom Development on NC

For: organisations wanting a specific system built on our stack.
You get: a working system, with source, built by the people who built the language.
Produces: software you own and can run without us.

Pilot-first

Where we start you in observe-only mode

Beta

Autonomous Operations Pilot

For: SRE and platform teams with a high volume of repetitive investigation.
How it runs: read-only and in shadow mode first. The agents investigate and propose; nothing touches your infrastructure until you have seen enough to extend that trust.
Produces: evidence of whether agent-led investigation is useful in your environment.

Beta

Agent Governance Pilot

For: teams giving agents write access to systems that matter.
How it runs: AGP sits in front of your existing agents in observe-only mode, recording what it would have refused without blocking anything, so budgets get calibrated against real traffic first.
Produces: a concrete picture of how often an input was steering a privileged action more than you assumed.
