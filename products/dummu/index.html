Dummu · Autonomous Platform

Dummu
One autonomous platform.

Dummu is an autonomous platform for platform engineering, SRE, and IT operations, bringing incident management, natural-language agent building, and continuous reinforcement learning together. Detect issues, recommend a response, and execute remediation within the authority your team grants.

Request a demonstrationExplore capabilities

Customer-hosted on Kubernetes · Dry-run by default · Staged autonomy

One operational workflow

Understand the incident. Control the response.

Incident intelligence

Ingest alerts and events, correlate related signals, and recommend remediation with confidence scores and preflight checks.

Governed remediation

Restart, scale, and roll back Kubernetes workloads through policy checks and required approvals. Dry-run, change freezes, and kill switches constrain execution.

Natural-language agent builder

Describe what an agent should do in plain English, generate its definition, then review its tools, namespace access, and action boundaries. Test, schedule, or trigger it by webhook, with approval required by default.

Accountability built in

People and agents share the same controls.

Risk assessment, policy checks, and approvals happen before execution. Separation of duties, single-use approvals, and a two-person rule support controlled changes. Hash-chained audit records and agent traces provide evidence for review.

Learning and autonomy

Grow autonomy in deliberate stages.

Observe, recommend, approve

Start with observation and recommendations, then enable approved execution for agreed action classes. Outcomes are recorded and confidence is updated to inform future recommendations.

Continuous reinforcement learning

Diagnostic policies update from completed incident outcomes. Resolution success, time taken, tool efficiency, and operator feedback inform future diagnostic choices. Learning changes recommendations within the configured governance boundaries.

Further autonomy levels, including self-adjusting execution authority, are on the roadmap. Learning from outcomes does not expand the authority you grant.

Deployment and evaluation

Run in your environment.

Deploy on customer-managed Kubernetes, with an AKS deployment kit and Docker Compose for local evaluation. Microsoft Entra ID provides sign-in and group-based roles. Connect alerting, collaboration, work management, and infrastructure integrations to the workflow.

AI provider choice. Governance and remediation work without an LLM. Optional AI features use the provider and credentials you configure; prompts may leave your cluster for that provider.

A scoped evaluation typically takes 4–6 weeks, with agreed incident types, approval controls, and success criteria. Measure recommendation acceptance, remediation time, and audit completeness against your baseline.

Client experience

Used in a client environment.

DevHeal reports that Dummu has reduced mean time to resolution (MTTR) for a client. A quantified case study, measurement period, and client identity are not published here. Evaluate results against your own incident baseline.
