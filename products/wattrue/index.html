Capabilities

One workflow, from the first bill to the portfolio view.

01

Bill audit

Read electricity, gas and LPG bills and check the charges against your tariff. Review findings with the amount and supporting source.

02

Tariff & load optimisation

Compare eligible tariff categories, contract demand and power-factor correction using your own bill data.

03

Anomaly detection

Spot unusual consumption with weather- and occupancy-adjusted comparisons, and investigate likely causes.

How it works

The evidence does the work.
Your team makes the call.

  1. Read & check

    Compare bill lines with your tariff, demand and penalties.

  2. Find & prioritise

    Rank tariff fixes and efficiency opportunities by impact.

  3. Approve & act

    Give your team a clear action, with a second person approving delivery.

  4. Measure & report

    Track results against a baseline and keep the source records connected.

Fits your workflow

Bring the data in. Keep the decision with your team.

Bills & business data

Upload PDFs or import CSV, Excel, ODS, JSON, XML and text. Forward bill attachments to the inbox or use API imports with preview and confirmation.

Team communication

Deliver approved tasks and alerts through email, Microsoft Teams, Slack and WhatsApp, with a record of approval and delivery.

Reports & portability

Export reporting data as CSV, HTML or Excel, and your workspace as JSON. Connect staff through Google sign-in; company single sign-on is available on the Company plan.

Evidence & control

Clear sources. Visible estimates. Human approval.

Trace the number

Follow a figure back to its bill, tariff or record. Estimates are identified and missing information stays visible.

Review before sending

A different colleague approves outbound tasks and alerts. Chat-created records wait for confirmation.

Understand the data flow

Documents are read on the Wattrue server. Relevant extracted text may be passed to the AI model to answer your question.
