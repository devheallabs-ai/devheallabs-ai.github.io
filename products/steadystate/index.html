Deployment and integration details

SteadyState brings together cloud and on-premises operations, including VMware, Kubernetes, AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. Adapter, resource type, permissions, and network connectivity determine the available actions.

AetherGraph supports orchestration and traces. Learning workflows use outcome memory, replay, and promotion gates. Production learning promotion remains gated; successful command execution and verified service recovery are recorded separately.
