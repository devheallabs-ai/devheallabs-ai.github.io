A few things to know.

What do the videos show?

The provided recordings show Mino’s software running in a 3D model. They are captioned and silent, and do not demonstrate finished physical hardware.

Does every feature work on the robot alone?

No. The design distinguishes on-device English commands from free-flowing conversation through a home computer hub. Hindi and Telugu conversation is shown through that hub.

Is Mino a medical or counselling product?

Mino is a companion concept, not a medical device or a substitute for professional care or human support.

Who is Mino designed for?

Adults and teens aged 14 and over. The website is not positioning Mino as a children’s toy. Launch-update requests should be made by adults.

How can I try Mino?

The web and mobile versions are available now. Contact DevHeal for access details. The physical robot is still in development; this page does not take robot orders or payments.
