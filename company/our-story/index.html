Our story · Hyderabad, India

Built on research.
Driven by real-world work.

DevHeal Labs AI began as an R&D initiative in 2024 in Hyderabad, India.

That foundation shapes our work today: exploring AI model architectures while engineering the systems, controls, and autonomous platforms that put intelligence to work.

One connected approach

Research at the model layer. Engineering at the systems layer.

01

AI model research

Our research explores sovereign AI architectures, sequence processing, and continual-learning foundations. Programmes include Nova-X CSLM, ORBIT, PRISM, Cognitive Crystal, UTL-C, and SEVK-C.

Explore our research →
02

AI systems engineering

We build the foundations around intelligence: programming tools, local inference, agent orchestration, governance, and bounded execution. This work spans NC, NC AI, AetherGraph, AGP, and AgentOS.

Autonomous operations
