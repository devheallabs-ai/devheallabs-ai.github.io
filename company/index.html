About DevHeal Labs

DevHeal Labs is building the engineering platform required to design, build, evaluate, simulate, deploy and operate reliable autonomous AI systems — the AI Systems Engineering category.

Mission

AI is moving from isolated models and chat interfaces toward systems composed of models, agents, tools, memory, data, policies, infrastructure and business workflows. DevHeal exists to build the engineering layer those systems need — starting with NC, the programming foundation, and expanding into simulation, evaluation, runtime and recovery.

Built in Hyderabad, India

"Founded on the conviction that building reliable AI systems needs an engineering discipline of its own — not just a bigger model or a cleverer prompt."
