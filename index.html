Featured product

Dummu

An autonomous platform for operations, agents, and continuous learning.

For SRE, platform engineering, and IT operations teams. Dummu connects incident signals to recommendations and approved remediation, and lets teams build operational agents from natural-language instructions, with tools and action boundaries they control.

Policy checks, approvals, dry-run, kill switches, and tamper-evident audit records sit in the execution path. Continuous reinforcement learning uses resolution outcomes, time taken, tool efficiency, and operator feedback to improve diagnostic choices.
