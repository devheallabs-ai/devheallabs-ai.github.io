Privacy and Cookie Notice

How DevHeal Labs AI handles website enquiries, technical data, external services and browser preferences.

Who we are

DevHeal Labs AI Private Limited (CIN U62011TS2026PTC219968), IDA Mallapur, Hyderabad, Telangana, India ("we"). This notice covers devheallabs.com. Our products have their own notices.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us: your name, email, company and what you write. The contact form prepares an email in your own email app, and we receive it only if you send it.
  • Technical data: IP address and request details, processed by our hosting and network providers to deliver the site and keep it secure.

How we use it

To reply to you, discuss our products and services, understand how the site is used, and keep it secure. We do not sell personal data.

Who receives it

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: network and security services.
  • GitHub, Inc.: website hosting through GitHub Pages.
  • Google LLC: Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when fonts load.
  • Our email provider, for messages you send us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers process data outside India, including in the United States.

Cookies

The website code does not set cookies. When you choose a light or dark theme, it stores that preference in your browser's local storage under devheal-theme. This preference stays on your device and can be removed through your browser's site-data settings. Your email app, browser and external services may keep their own records.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and manage the relationship, then delete them unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your rights

Subject to law, you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, name someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. Contact info@devheallabs.com. Where available under applicable law, you may also complain to the competent data protection authority, including the Data Protection Board of India.

Grievance Officer

Grievance Officer, DevHeal Labs AI Private Limited, IDA Mallapur, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. Email: info@devheallabs.com.
