How to read this assessment.

This is a source-review coverage map. “Partial demonstration” means only part of a requirement is illustrated. A file-presence check is not proof that a control works. No complete-conformance or certification claim is made.

The specification requires mandatory requirements to be satisfied or formally excepted; the requirements list distinguishes Critical and High priority. Record the interpretation, responsible approver and any accepted exception with your assessment.
