Enterprise engagement model

A practical path from pilot to production.

Work with our engineering team to scope deployment boundaries, identity integration, and measurable success criteria.

Private deployment architecture

Deploy inside your AKS/Kubernetes cluster or on-prem servers with explicit network egress and AI provider boundaries.

Workflow & toolchain integration

Connect PagerDuty, Prometheus, Kubernetes APIs, Slack/Teams, and internal runbooks without exposing production credentials to public clouds.
